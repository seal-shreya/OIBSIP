The little earth approach

Good for your routine.
Gentler on the planet.

Sustainability doesn't have to be all-or-nothing. These three ideas make a friendly place to begin.

🫙

Choose reusables

Keep a bottle, cup, or tote you already own close by. The easiest reusable is the one you'll remember to bring.

🌼

Buy with intention

Pause before a purchase. Borrow, repair, or shop what you already have before buying something new.

🌱

Let habits grow

Pick one manageable change and repeat it. Small steps are easier to keep than an overnight routine overhaul.

🍃
Our idea

Less pressure.

Little Earth is a concept for a more approachable kind of sustainable living. It's about making room for better habits without chasing perfection or replacing everything you own.

Use what you have, care for it for longer, and choose your next step when you're ready.

Your tiny next step

Pick one. Keep it easy.

Choose a habit that fits your real life — not an imaginary perfect one.

👜

Carry a bag

Leave a reusable bag near your keys or in your backpack so it's ready when you need it.

🥕

Use what you have

Plan one meal around ingredients already in your kitchen and help reduce avoidable food waste.

💧

Refill and repeat

Keep your water bottle visible and refill it as part of your daily routine.
